# Amendment 01 to pre-registration 0001

Date: 2026-11-06. Written before any result exists: no test in 0001 has been run, and no statistic has been computed from the real data.

Three clarifications. None changes a rule, a tolerance or a verdict.

1. Partial years. Calendar-year figures use complete calendar years only. After publication, 2026 runs only to September (END = 2026-09), so it is reported separately as a partial year (January to September) and is used in no calendar-year figure or verdict.
2. The family. Its monthly return is the average of the 15 versions' returns in that month: the same as holding equal amounts in each version, reset every month.
3. Drift weights, sensitivity check only. In the first holding month, every held sector starts at equal weight.
